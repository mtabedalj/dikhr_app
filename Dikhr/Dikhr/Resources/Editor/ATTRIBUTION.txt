Dikhr — Resources/Editor/ attribution
=====================================

The My Athkar rich-text editor in Dikhr is built on the RichEditorView
WebView editor, copied from the Athkar app bundle
(/Applications/Athkar.app/Wrapper/athkar.app).

Copied files (renamed so they stay unique inside the Dikhr target):

  rich_editor.js    -> RichEditorScript.js
  rich_editor.html  -> RichEditorPage.html  (page shell adapted; see comment block)
  style.css         -> RichEditorStyle.css
  normalize.css     -> RichEditorNormalize.css
